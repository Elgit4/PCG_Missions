# Church on Missions International - CMI Follow-up

**Guide version:** 2.1 | **Aligned with:** CMI Follow-up Master Product Specification v2.1

This guide describes the intended operating workflows. A feature is available only after it has been implemented, configured and enabled for your account; SMS additionally requires an approved provider and sender identity.

*Internal guide for authorized team members*

This guide explains how to use the CMI Follow-up app for field capture, follow-up, events, prayer, and visitor-facing content.

Use this guide with the role and permissions assigned to your account. The specific pages and actions available to you depend on your role and assignment.

---

## 1. Getting started and sign-in

The app supports the full follow-up workflow for a service, outreach, or event - from capture and assignment to care, prayer, and reports.

Sign in with the email and PIN provided by your administrator. Your access is limited to the pages and records permitted for your role.

If you use a shared device, sign out when finished. Do not share your PIN.

**Open:** Sign in

---

## 2. Contacts and field capture

Contacts are the core record for visitors and members in the app. Each contact can carry its own notes, follow-up history, and assignment.

Add a contact from the Contacts page or from the event workspace. Required fields are minimal for fast field entry; details can be added later.

Capture contacts online, or use the queued offline capture form if connection is unavailable. Queued captures sync when connection is restored.

Use the provided capture pickers to record source, area, age range, and other team-configured options.

Merge duplicate contacts from the record's actions menu when two entries describe the same person.

**Open:** Contacts . Add contact . Queue

---

## 3. Contacts, lists, and follow-up

Keep contact work organized through lists and assignment.

Create a follow-up list from contacts or from the follow-up page, then assign it to a team member. Assignees see the list under their work.

A follow-up item may include a scheduled date, priority, and notes. Complete an item after contact has been made. Overdue items remain visible until cleared or reassigned.

Records remain scoped to your permissions. Lists, filters, and contact visibility are restricted to the contacts and teams you are permitted to view.

**Open:** Contacts . Follow-up

---

## 4. Prayer and care

Prayer requests and care notes are held under restricted access. Treat them as private pastoral information.

Add a prayer request from the Prayer page or from the linked contact. You may set an expiry date to limit how long the request remains visible.

A request assigned to a prayer intercessor is visible only to that intercessor and to permitted administrators. Unassigned requests remain visible to the roles permitted to review them.

Mark a request answered, update it, or remove it as needed. Do not share prayer details outside permitted roles and channels.

**Open:** Prayer

---

## 5. Care and contacts directory

The Care page summarizes the people who need attention and helps you reach them through permitted channels.

Add and maintain care contacts and groups. Depending on your permissions, you can view care summaries and update a person's follow-up status.

Use the Care workspace for follow-up calls and visits. Record an outcome and update status rather than creating duplicate records.

**Open:** Care

---

## 6. Events and registrations

Events support check-in, registration, follow-up, and closeout for services, outreaches, and meetings.

Create an event with date, location, and capacity, and optionally enable registration and transport reservations.

Attendees can register through the public event page when registration is enabled. Transport reservations are recorded as part of the registration.

Use the event check-in page to record attendance, then use the event workspace for assigned follow-up.

Share public event links and QR codes as provided by the app.

**Open:** Events . Event check-in

---

## 7. Church and event calendar

The calendar brings together services, scheduled follow-up, and prayer follow-ups.

Add or update service calendar entries from the calendar or event tools. Entries created by the team can include service and location details.

The calendar supports scheduling follow-up, prayer, and event tasks within the assigned team.

**Open:** Calendar

---

## 8. Announcements and SMS communication

Authorized staff can create announcements for church, team or event audiences within their permissions. Available delivery channels depend on deployment configuration. Your account may also have access to one or more of these SMS functions:

- **Individual SMS:** compose and send a message to one permitted contact.
- **Bulk SMS:** prepare a message for an authorized audience, review the recipient count and message, then confirm the campaign if your role permits.
- **Automated SMS:** the system may send approved messages for enabled events such as registration confirmation, event updates/reminders or scheduled follow-up reminders.

Before sending, check the recipient number and message. Respect consent and communication preferences. Contacts who have opted out or are suppressed must not receive non-essential messages. Bulk campaigns may show an estimated recipient count, message segments and cost where available.

Message statuses may include queued, submitted, delivered, failed or undeliverable. Submitted does not necessarily mean delivered. Report failed messages to an authorized administrator rather than repeatedly sending them.

Never include prayer-request details, private pastoral notes, confidential care information, PINs or passwords in an SMS. WhatsApp links are user-initiated and are not automated WhatsApp messaging.

**Open:** Announcements . SMS / Communications

---

## 9. Sermons and daily words

Sermons and daily words can be prepared for public display.

Create a sermon or daily word from the relevant page. Add a title, content, and applicable date.

A draft remains private until published from the administration page. Published items become visible to visitors on the public church site.

**Open:** Sermons . Daily words

---

## 10. Public visitor site and content

The visitor site is separate from staff pages and does not require a staff sign-in.

The public church home shares church information and upcoming active events, with links to event details, registrations, and configured transport reservations.

Authorized staff can update the church profile, visitor description, WhatsApp contact, and social links shown to visitors.

Publish or hide sermons and daily words from the Visitor content administration page. Published items appear on the public church site.

Publish mobile-friendly digital tracts, then share their public links and QR codes. An optional PDF link can point to an existing public HTTPS file; PDF file hosting is not provided here.

Visitors can register for events, read available welcome messages and tracts, view published church content, and contact the church using available links.

**Open:** Visitor content

---

## 11. Reports and team administration

Reporting and account administration are permission-controlled.

Submit daily evangelism, event evangelism, or weekly ministry reports. Depending on role, reports may be reviewed, approved, or returned for changes.

View reports within your authorized scope and use event reports/closeout for attendance and event operations.

The Team page lists active and inactive members. Authorized administrators can add members, assign roles and area coverage, edit access, and deactivate accounts.

The Events workspace supports event-specific team assignments. Event roles limit staff access to the relevant event.

Administrators can maintain the church profile, service areas, visitor-facing content, and other settings in the relevant administration screens.

**Open:** Reports . Team . Church settings

---

## 12. Roles at a glance

A user may have more than one role; their effective permissions are combined. Actual record access remains scoped to the church, team, event, or assignment.

**Admin / Pastor:** church-wide administration, team and settings management, exports, oversight, and privileged care workflows.

**Coordinator:** event coordination, church contact access, follow-up, reports, announcements, prayer management, and permitted team communication.

**Team leader:** team-scoped contacts and reports, event management, follow-up, and team communication.

**Field worker:** field capture and own contact/report access.

**Follow-up volunteer:** assigned/own contact follow-up and reports.

**Prayer intercessor:** private messages plus prayer requests specifically assigned to them.

---

## 13. Important limits and future work

The following items are not currently available as complete app features. Do not promise them as enabled services.

SMS is supported for authorized individual messages, bulk campaigns and explicitly enabled automated triggers. SMS availability depends on approved provider configuration, sender identity, consent and account permissions. WhatsApp automation is not included.

Discipleship lesson paths, disengagement/retention alerts, territory coverage maps, response maps, testimonies/milestones feeds, and printed-tract inventory are future or held items.

The optional seeker-facing AI tools, interactive gospel journey, anonymous Q&A, and feedback surveys are not currently provided.

Multi-church network administration is not enabled. The current service is configured for the church instance and branch.

Offline mode is limited to queued field captures, cached capture pickers, and previously opened event check-in data. It is not a full offline copy of the application.

---

## 14. Privacy and safe use

The app stores sensitive pastoral and contact information. Use the least access needed and follow church policy.

Do not share a PIN or leave an admin session open on a shared device. Sign out when finished and use a device lock.

Only collect information needed for ministry follow-up. Respect the person's consent and keep prayer requests private unless sharing was explicitly approved.

Use role- and event-scoped access rather than sending attendee lists or private details through public channels.

Use the application's consent prompts as configured by the church. Data-retention and data-subject request processes must follow the church's approved policy.

For problems, record the page, approximate time, and action that failed, then contact the church's app administrator. Never send passwords, PINs, or private visitor data in a support request.
